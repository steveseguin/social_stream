# Original SSN sounds

Browse and listen in [Sound Library](../../audio-library.html). In Event Flow, choose **Play Audio Clip → Browse sounds**. In Multi-Alerts settings, use **Browse sounds** beside the sound you want to change. Previews play locally; live Event Flow audio plays through the Flow Actions browser source.

The original sound files in this directory are offered under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). You may use, modify, and redistribute them, including in monetized streams. Attribution is optional.

The library includes effects and prerecorded phrases in English, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese, with friendly, upbeat, and playful delivery. Playing these files does not load a speech model or call a speech service.

The voices were generated with [Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign), without reference recordings or voice cloning. The effects offered in the browser were generated with [MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-SoundEffect-v2.0). Both models use Apache-2.0 licenses, separate from the audio files. Older procedural effects remain available at their existing file URLs. No third-party sound clips were imported.
