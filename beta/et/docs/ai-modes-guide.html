Connect an AI in 5 steps

Every option except the ChatGPT tab needs this first.

  1. In the Social Stream popup, open Chat Bots and AI services.
  2. Avatud Configure LLM Service Provider and pick the AI you use.
  3. Fill in its address, model, API key, or download settings.
  4. Klõpsake Test selected chat bot.
  5. Turn on the feature that should use it, like the Primary bot or translation.
Connected isn't the finish line. It only means the AI answered the test. Nothing reads your chat until you turn on a feature.

Voice co-host

Talk to an AI on stream. It hears your microphone, sees chat you share, and can answer out loud.

  1. Avatud cohost.html?session=YOUR_SESSION.
  2. Pick a voice AI, or the AI you set up in the popup.
  3. Pick your microphone and Audio Response, then press Start Co-host.
  4. With OpenAI Realtime, capture the co-host page audio in OBS.
  5. Lisa cohost-overlay.html?session=YOUR_SESSION to OBS for the avatar and speech bubble.
Live Chat settingMida see teeb

None of these post into platform chat. Keep the speech bubble visible so viewers can read along.

OpenAI Realtime details
  • Uses the OpenAI key saved under ChatGPT API. The key stays in Social Stream. The co-host page only gets a short-lived pass, and audio travels over WebRTC.
  • The overlay doesn't get the OpenAI voice. Use the co-host output picker for a virtual cable if supported. &forcetts adds a second, separate overlay voice.
  • For speed: Mini model, Low reasoning, Fast turn-taking. Full quality and higher reasoning are smarter but slower and cost more. Fast can cut in on pauses.
  • Camera and Screen Share are optional. Default is No Video. When on, it sends one picture per request, not live video. This adds cost and delay.
  • Spotify, OBS scene, and featured-chat tools are off by default. Turn them on in the popup, then in Co-host Stream Controls.
  • Replies are capped at 512 output tokens. Diagnostics shows usage and delay.
  • It reconnects by itself. A fresh session keeps your instructions and settings, but forgets what was said aloud.
  • It doesn't take over a conversation in the ChatGPT website or app.

Primary chat bot

Answers viewer messages on an overlay, out loud with TTS, or back in chat where supported.

  1. Connect an AI and test it.
  2. Avatud Chat Bot - Primary and turn on Enable the LLM AI chat bot.
  3. Set trigger words, moderators-only, rate limits, history, and instructions.
  4. For a safe first test, turn on Bot replies ONLY go to the bot overlay page.
  5. Avatud bot.html?session=YOUR_SESSION. Turn on TTS if replies should be spoken.
  6. Turn overlay-only off only after you've checked that platform supports posting.

Replies post from your logged-in account. For a separate name, use a YouTube, Twitch, or Kick bot account where supported.

ChatGPT tab (experimental)

Already use ChatGPT in Chrome and don't want an API key? Social Stream can pick up new messages from your ChatGPT tab, like any other chat source (type openai).

Capture your ChatGPT tab

  1. Install and turn on the Social Stream Ninja Chrome extension.
  2. In the popup, turn on the ChatGPT source.
  3. Avatud https://chatgpt.com/, then reload it.
  4. Open the Dock with the same session.
  5. Send a new ChatGPT message. Both sides should show in the Dock. Older messages are skipped on purpose.

Send your live chat into ChatGPT

  1. Test on a private or unlisted stream, in a normal text chat.
  2. Under Management, turn on Relay all messages.
  3. Määra relay destinations juurde openai, so viewer messages only go to ChatGPT.
  4. Keep the tab open. ChatGPT's replies come back as openai messages.
  5. Use ChatGPT's audio, or Social Stream TTS or an overlay, to put replies on stream.
Experimental. The ChatGPT site can change. Relaying all chat can flood the conversation. Voice mode may not accept typed-in text. Use filters or a test stream first.
