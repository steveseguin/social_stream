or logos/wordmark-dark-text.png on a light background.

For words to read or paste: open AD-COPY.txt.

IN OBS

Add a PNG as an Image source. Transparent logos and the lower third can
sit over gameplay or camera footage. Scale proportionally and place the
lower third where it will not cover game controls or existing captions.

Add the MP4 as a Media Source. Enable "Restart playback when source
becomes active" for a promo scene. It is 15 seconds, 1920x1080, 30 fps,
H.264, and silent. Add your own voiceover or music you have permission to
use. Leave Loop off for a one-time ad; turn it on for a repeating card.

Use the logo in its original proportions with clear space around it.

editable/ contains SVG layouts with editable text and embedded artwork.
The mascot and campaign illustration are raster images inside the SVGs.
Text uses Segoe UI, with Arial as a fallback. Fonts are not bundled;
check wrapping after opening an SVG in your design app. Use the PNGs
when you want the supplied layout exactly as shown.

originals/ contains the existing square project logo and mascot artwork.
The transparent mascot and dimensional campaign art offer alternate
presentations for ads. Choose one presentation consistently in your edit.

Use these assets in creator videos, streams and posts promoting Social

community shout-out as a paid sponsorship unless one actually exists.
