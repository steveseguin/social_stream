玩法說明

輸入 !quick 加入。被點名後，在 30 秒內輸入自己的代碼。答對後會重新排隊；錯過回合則需要重新加入。疊加畫面會列出個人最佳時間最快的五名玩家。代碼不區分大小寫。

Transparent OBS background. Refreshing the browser source starts fresh. Scores stay only in this page. No bot messages, points, or purchases. Times include stream and chat delay, so this is a casual competition. Random codes discourage prepared answers; they cannot prevent automated reading.

三種全新玩法

捕捉螢火蟲：輸入 !catch 開始，等螢火蟲進入圓環時捕捉。每位觀眾每次飛行只能嘗試一次；在中心捕獲得 3 分，在圓環其他位置捕獲得 1 分。飛行六次後決出勝者。

彗星競速：輸入 !boost 加入自動分配的隊伍。每位觀眾每 3 秒可助推一次。先達到 20 次助推的一方獲勝，或在 60 秒後由領先一方獲勝。

迷宮突襲：速度更快的自動探索者會在帶有 SSN 牆面標誌的磚石和金屬紋理走廊中移動。普通聊天訊息會為每位發言者產生一個頭像敵人，每回合最多 40 個。突襲者會在相隔 3 到 8 格走廊的位置產生，並進行追逐和攻擊；完成最後一擊者獲勝。頭像圖片載入失敗或缺少時會顯示姓名首字母。迷宮會填滿 OBS 瀏覽器來源；請調整來源的大小以配合場景。

All three restart after an 8-second result screen. No points are spent and no bot messages are sent.

No commands needed

Games that run on hype

Six transparent overlays that react to how excited chat is, not to what it types. Each one measures chat against what is normal for your channel, so a five-person chat and a five-thousand-person chat both get a fair game. Donations, subs, follows and raids count as bigger jolts.

Six ways to spend the excitement

Chat Quake: a live trace along the bottom of the stream. A sudden rush of chat is flagged as a quake, with a magnitude and the word chat was shouting, and the biggest one is kept as the record. Add &style=paper for a paper chart in the corner, or add &theme=night to darken the chart. Inspired by Matthew Busel's ChatQuake.

Quake City: a small skyline along the bottom. Excited chat cracks and topples the buildings one at a time. Flatten it and it is rebuilt a level taller and tougher, so the next knockdown takes more chat.

Hype Flight: chat keeps a little bird in the air through wide, easy gaps. More chat flies higher, the bird ducks under clouds by itself, and only a lull makes it miss.

Chat Volcano: chat builds pressure until it erupts, and the eruption lasts as long as the hype does. The mountain grows after each one.

Hype Rocket: chat fuels a rocket up the side of the screen, from the Cloud Deck to the Moon and beyond. Each destination is farther than the last.

Chat Campfire: every message is a log on the fire. A chat keeping its usual pace holds a steady campfire, excitement builds a bonfire, and silence lets it die down to embers.

Options: &sensitivity=low 或 high, &scale=1.5, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick, &hud=0 to hide the text, &shake=0 for no shaking, &color=ff8800，以及 &align=left, center 或 right to move the volcano, rocket or campfire. The seismograph also takes &window=120 for the seconds shown and &bg=0 to drop its panel.

Built for the stream, not the page

Portrait games, team towers and show-time scenes

Seven games drawn to be watched at stream size. They show no titles and no instructions: only the game, the numbers that matter, and the one thing to type.

For portrait streams (9:16)

Peg Drop：輸入 !drop to drop your ball from the moving chute. The pegs decide where it lands, and the gold slot pays triple. Chat Blocks: a falling-block puzzle the whole chat steers. Type a column number, 1 為 8, to vote where the block lands, or spin to turn it. Sky Drop：輸入 !jump to parachute out of the airship, then left 和 right to steer against the wind onto the target. In a 16:9 scene these three keep their portrait shape; add &align=left 或 right to move them.

Two teams, clear middle

Tower Duel: every chatter is put on one of two teams automatically. Each message drops a crate from that team's crane, and the first tower to reach the flag wins. In a 16:9 scene the towers use only the sides, so a 9:16 video fits between them untouched.

Before, during a break, and after the show

Front Row: a starting-soon countdown. The first message from each chatter drops them into a seat, and the crowd celebrates at zero. Keep It Up: for breaks. Every message bumps the balloon; chat tries to beat its longest rally. Encore: for the end of the show. Every chatter launches a firework, and a busy chat sets off a finale.

Options: &scale=0.8, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. Peg Drop &round=180 (seconds, 0 for endless). Chat Blocks &turn=8 (seconds to vote). Sky Drop &round=75. Tower Duel &goal=12 (crates to win), &gap=0.3 (share of the width left clear), &panel=0 (no backing), &command=!build (only that command drops). Front Row &minutes=5 或 &until=19:30. Keep It Up &minutes=5 to show a break timer. Encore &crowd=8 (chatters needed for the finale).

Faces first

Races, a boss, a quiz, and a crowd of your chatters

Eleven more pages built around your chatters' own pictures and names. As before: no titles, no instructions, only the game and the one thing to type.

播放（Play）

Marble Race：輸入 !play and your marble, wearing your picture, races everyone else down an obstacle course that is different every time. Built for portrait. Cliff Climb: every message is one hop up the cliff, and the first to the flag wins. It uses only the two sides of a 16:9 scene, so a 9:16 video fits in the middle. Stream Boss: every message chips at the boss. Tips, subs and raids hit hard, members hit half again as hard, and whoever lands the final blow becomes the next boss, with their own picture in the frame. The boss heals a little whenever they chat. Trivia: a quiz show. Type a, b, c 或 d; faster correct answers score more. Ten question packs are built in, or load your own. Stream Bingo: one bingo card for the whole stream, with a picture in every square. When that thing happens, chat types the square's number; enough callers stamp it, and a full line is a bingo.

Always on

Chat Crowd: everyone who chats becomes a little character with their own picture, strolling along the bottom of the stream. They hop when their owner talks, show the first emoji from the message, and obey !jump, !wave, !dance, !hug 和 !duel (add a name to pick who).

Before, during a break, and after the show

Roll Call: a starting-soon countdown where everyone who says hello lands on a growing wall of faces. Chat Aquarium: a calm break screen where every chatter is a fish, and a tip drops treasure for the whole tank. Group Photo: an end-of-show class photo. Everyone who says goodbye walks into frame, the camera flashes, and the picture drops out as a print. Winners' Podium: the stream's top players across every game. The games on this page award stars (5, 3 and 2 for the top three of a round, 1 for taking part); the podium adds them up. Chatters type !stars to see their own total. Stars are kept in the browser source's own storage, per session, and start again after twelve quiet hours.

One source, many games

Game Rotation: one browser source that plays a list of games in turn and cross-fades between them. Add &games=marblerace,trivia,pegdrop to choose the list, &every=5 for the minutes each one stays, and &vote to let chat pick the next game by typing 1, 2 或 3. Every other option on the address is passed on to each game.

Options for every game in this section and the one above: &membersonly (only members and subscribers, and anyone sending a paid message, take part), &scale=0.8, &align=left, &calm (no screen shake or flashes), &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. They slow themselves down on a busy computer rather than slow the stream: first a lower drawing resolution, then fewer frames. &fps=30 或 &quality=0.75 holds a setting, and &fps=full 會關閉此功能。

Marble Race &lobby=25 (seconds to join), &max=60 (marbles), &command=play. Cliff Climb &goal=20 (hops to the top), &gap=0.3, &command=climb. Stream Boss &hp=1000 (leave it out and the boss grows with the crowd), &align=bottom left. Trivia &pack=general,science (general, science, geography, history, movies, music, gaming, animals, food, sports), &time=20, &rounds=10, &questions=https://... (your own JSON list of [question, right answer, wrong, wrong, wrong], on a host that allows cross-site requests). Stream Bingo &votes=3 (callers needed; a moderator's call counts at once), &lines=1, &squares=Laughs|Pet appears|.... Chat Crowd &max=30, &idle=10 (minutes before a silent character leaves). Roll Call &minutes=5 或 &until=19:30. Chat Aquarium &minutes=10 (break timer), &idle=10, &water=0 (fish only, over your own break screen). Group Photo &every=45 (seconds between photos), &crowd=12. Winners' Podium &top=8, &season=12 (hours; 0 keeps no scores).

The hype games (Chat Quake, Quake City, Hype Flight, Chat Volcano, Hype Rocket, Chat Campfire) take &skin=winter, spooky, autumn, spring, love 或 party for a seasonal look. &membersonly also works on the hype games, the bottom-strip games, Quick Call and the card games further down this page. Tug of War, Rock Paper Showdown, Minority Club, Meteor Shield, Beacon Relay, Crew Kitchen, Sum Squad, Memory Parade and Odd One Out are now drawn as scenes with your chatters' pictures in them; their commands and rules are unchanged.

01 / 一起解題

訊號解鎖

1?4?

聊天室有 90 秒破解四位數字。每次猜測都會給所有人一條線索。適合解謎愛好者、學習空檔、遊戲大廳和小型社群。

玩法說明

輸入 !code 1234。數字範圍為 1 到 6，可以重複。「Exact」表示數字和位置都正確。另一個計數表示數字正確但位置不同。

第一次有效猜測啟動計時。每位觀眾兩次猜測之間需間隔五秒；重複的猜測會被忽略。

02 / 讓聊天室決定

眾人探險

由聊天室引導、途經五站的太空之旅。選擇路線，管理共用物資，帶著發現返航。輕鬆的節奏適合聊天、藝術、音樂、休閒遊戲和綜合類直播。

玩法說明

輸入 !vote 1, !vote 2，或 !vote 3。每個章節給船員 30 秒作出選擇。每位觀眾一票；最新的有效選擇會取代先前的投票。

每章由第一張選票啟動。投票前會顯示可能結果。票數相同則隨機決定；所需物資多於船員現有物資的路線不可選。

兩支隊伍 / 一條繩子

拔河

兩個友善的小怪物分別代表珊瑚隊和薄荷隊拉繩。隊友答對題目，繩子就會向你們這一邊移動。

體驗預覽 →

玩法說明

輸入 !join ，系統會將你分到人數較少的一隊（人數相同時隨機分配）。兩隊都有玩家後比賽開始。每場比賽內隊伍保持固定；歡迎中途加入。

每 12 秒出現一個單字或簡單算術題。直接在聊天中輸入單字或答案，或使用 !answer ，後面接你的答案。每名玩家每道題最多獲得一次拉動；重複作答不會增加拉動次數。

領先 20 次拉動即可獲勝。否則，三分鐘後領先的一方獲勝；繩子居中則為平手。結果畫面結束後，觀眾重新組隊進行下一場。不會消耗忠誠度點數。

保護飛船，抵禦六波流星。讓護盾顏色與來襲流星相符。

體驗預覽 →

玩法說明

輸入 !shield red.

每波每位觀眾可放置一個護盾，每秒可更換一次軌道。每個護盾可阻擋一顆顏色相符的流星；每顆未被擋住的流星會扣除一點船體值。流星波次會隨船員人數調整，每波最多 12 顆。

聊天之外

讓觀眾作決定，由你來實現：選擇下一種繪畫顏色、從候選清單中選下一首歌，或決定何時伸展休息。SSN 現有的投票、佇列和主播核准的貼圖獎勵，也提供其他參與方式。請保密工作階段連結和控制連結；觀眾只需要知道聊天中的操作說明。
