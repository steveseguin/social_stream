Start your first break

  1. Turn on Social Stream and enable remote API control in its settings. Open Monetization > Open Breaks. This opens your private workspace link.
  2. 選擇 Set up. Select a sports preset, use numbered spots, or paste one spot name per line. Spreadsheet columns can be pasted in this order: name, asking price, description, private note.
  3. Enter a title, buyer platform, and currency, then choose Create break. Assignments are manual on every platform. If using local stock, select an item to use one unit when creating the break.
  4. 在 Run break, select a spot, enter its buyer, and choose Save spot. A buyer name claims an available spot.
  5. 選擇 預覽 to check the audience view, then 複製 OBS 連結 and add it as a Browser Source. New boards stay hidden until you choose 顯示疊加畫面（Show overlay）。使用 顯示 to change their appearance.

Keep the private link private

Breaks has two links. The OBS overlay link shows viewers the public board. The private control link opens this workspace, with its payments, notes, costs and stock. Use it for your own windows and OBS docks, and never as a Browser Source or in a chat.

Breaks records are encrypted between the workspace and your SSN host, using a key that only the private link holds. Your session ID cannot read or change them, and the key is never sent over the network. The overlay is different: like every SSN overlay, it trusts its session, so someone with your session ID could send it false content. They still cannot see or change your records. If the private link leaks, choose Monetization > Reset private link. Old links stop receiving updates and cannot save. Reopen Breaks from Monetization to use the new link.

Encryption needs HTTPS, localhost or the app. A workspace opened from a plain HTTP address on your network cannot connect.

During the break

使用 Highlight to call attention to a spot. Enter the latest pull and choose 揭曉 to show it. Each spot shows one latest pull; changes remain in its activity log. Card image URLs go under Description and private note. To free a sold spot, set it to 可用 and save: this clears its buyer, payment, pull and private note, and the activity log keeps who held it.

Payment and price holds your asking price, confirmed paid amount, and payment status. Check payment on your selling platform before manually marking a spot paid. Enter the confirmed amount, including zero for a free spot. Use asking price copies the asking price into the amount field for review.

Unsaved spot, appearance, cost and stock edits stay in the workspace when switching views or refreshing. Choose Discard edits to load saved values. Keep the workspace open until edits are saved. On narrow screens, use Back to spots 或 Save and next.

Swap buyers exchanges owners, and each buyer keeps their payment record and private note. Undo reverses the last spot change, and hovering over it shows what that was; it leaves stage, visibility, lock and appearance alone. Lock prevents assignment edits until unlocked.

Add spots while filling. Only empty, available spots can be removed. Stage and visibility controls affect the Breaks display; they do not change your selling platform or OBS scenes.

使用 顯示 for grid, compact-strip, or side-column layouts, colors, and optional buyer names or asking prices. Available-only display hides taken spots without removing their records. For many results or long names, use Grid or a taller Browser Source.

The board shows how many spots are left and how many are claimed. Claimed counts include revealed spots and do not confirm payment. The stage label (Spots open, Breaking or Complete) follows the Stage you choose; a full board does not change it.

選擇 標準 或 Team colors ；對應的設定項目： Display > Theme. League presets fill in team codes and colors. Edit or clear them under Team code and colors in the spot editor. In Team colors, an image link appears beside the spot name in place of its code.

Finish and reuse

Finish and review opens a recap grouped by buyer, with payment status and the latest pull. In History, Run again prepares the selected break for a new show without its buyers, payments or stock usage.

Export CSV on Run break and Private CSV in History download assignments grouped by buyer, including payment amounts and private notes. Buyer recap CSV includes only the break, spots, buyers and latest pulls. Keep this file for your own sorting records.

Archive current break in History finishes the break and hides its board. Starting another break also offers to archive the current one; a break nothing has happened on is discarded instead of saved. History keeps up to 30 breaks. When it is full, use Export full record to keep everything a break holds, including stock records, costs and activity, then remove it. The CSV only covers spots. Each break keeps its latest 500 activity entries. Everything has to fit through the session relay, so very long lists of notes, saved setups or stock can reach a limit; removing some makes room.

使用 Display > Save this setup to save up to 12 setups. Setups preserve spot names, asking prices, images, team codes and colors, and appearance, including the theme; buyers, payment records, results, and private notes are cleared. Use current setup prepares a fresh copy of the current spot list.

Stock and costs

Stock keeps up to 100 local items with quantities and unit costs. It is optional and only there for breaks: it does not handle shipping or orders. Set up break copies an item's name and currency into the new-break form and selects one unit under Use stock. Creating the break uses that unit; choose None to create it without stock.

To open something from stock, use Stock used ；對應的設定項目： Run break，或 Use 1 in this break 在 Stock. This reduces the local quantity and records the item, quantity and unit cost on the break. Return to stock reverses it, including from a saved break under History. Undo does not touch stock, and a removed item comes back when returned.

To connect eBay, open Social Stream's 營利 settings and use its eBay seller connection. Once connected, the workspace shows your cached listings. Copy a listing into a stock draft, review quantity, and enter your own cost. Listing prices are sale prices. Local edits do not update eBay listings or stock.

Whatnot, eBay Live, TikTok, and other shows can use manual assignments. Breaks does not automatically match purchases, randomizer results, payments, or refunds to spots.

The private estimate adds the cost of stock used to Product cost outside stock and your fees. Enter only products not already recorded through stock in that manual cost field. Revenue uses host-confirmed paid amounts; missing amounts or unpriced stock make the estimate incomplete. All amounts use the break's currency; stock with no unit cost or in another currency is listed as not counted, and no exchange-rate conversion is applied.

Optional controls and games

Links under OBS and audience tools open existing games, polls, timers, and waitlists with your session. Keep audience games separate from purchase assignments and use formats allowed by your selling platform.

Event Flow 的 Commerce control action can highlight a spot, change the stage, and show or hide the overlay. It cannot read Breaks records or change buyers, payments, costs, or what the overlay shows publicly, because flows can be started through your session. Use "boardId":"active" so a flow keeps working from break to break.

{"boardId":"active","id":"1"}

Use that example with breakHighlight。對於 breakStage，使用 stage 帶有 filling, breaking，或 finished。對於 breakSettings，使用 visible 帶有 true 或 false. Use existing Flow Actions for sounds and celebrations.

Remote commands

The session API does not accept Breaks commands, because the session ID also appears in overlay links.

Records stay on the SSN installation hosting the session. Keep that host running for controls and overlays. Export completed breaks before changing installations or clearing app data. The workspace contains private records; do not use its URL as a viewer overlay.
