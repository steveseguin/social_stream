Quick Call(빠른 응답)

이름 세 개와 매번 새로운 네 글자 코드. 내 차례가 오면 준비하세요.

미리보기 체험 →

플레이 방법

입력: !quick 를 입력해 참가하세요. 호명되면 30초 안에 본인의 코드를 입력하세요. 맞게 답하면 대기열 뒤로 돌아가며 차례를 놓치면 다시 참가해야 합니다. 오버레이는 개인 최고 기록이 가장 빠른 다섯 명을 표시합니다. 코드는 대소문자를 구분하지 않습니다.

Transparent OBS background. Refreshing the browser source starts fresh. Scores stay only in this page. No bot messages, points, or purchases. Times include stream and chat delay, so this is a casual competition. Random codes discourage prepared answers; they cannot prevent automated reading.

OBS 레이어에 맞춘 설계

방송 화면 위에서 플레이

Firefly Catch and Comet Rally leave the surrounding page transparent; Maze Raid fills its source. Start with a 1280 × 800 browser source. Preview pages show a checkerboard to make the transparent areas visible.

새로운 플레이 방식 세 가지

Firefly Catch(반딧불이 잡기): 입력: !catch 로 시작한 다음 반딧불이가 고리 안에 들어오면 잡으세요. 한 번 날아갈 때 시청자당 한 번 시도할 수 있으며 중앙에서 잡으면 3점, 고리 안의 다른 위치에서 잡으면 1점입니다. 여섯 번 날아간 뒤 승자가 정해집니다.

Comet Rally(혜성 경주): 입력: !boost 를 입력하면 자동으로 배정되는 팀에 참가합니다. 시청자마다 3초에 한 번 가속할 수 있습니다. 먼저 20번 가속하거나 60초 뒤 앞선 팀이 승리합니다.

Maze Raid(미로 습격): 더 빠른 자동 탐험가가 SSN 벽 표지판이 있는 벽돌과 금속 질감의 복도를 탐색합니다. 일반 채팅은 채팅 참여자마다 아바타 적 하나를 생성하며 라운드당 최대 40명입니다. 침입자는 복도 3~8칸 거리에 나타나 추격하고 공격하며, 마지막 타격을 가한 사람이 승리합니다. 아바타 이미지를 불러오지 못하거나 이미지가 없으면 이니셜을 표시합니다. 미로는 OBS 브라우저 소스 전체를 채우므로 장면에 맞게 소스 크기를 조절하세요.

All three restart after an 8-second result screen. No points are spent and no bot messages are sent.

No commands needed

Games that run on hype

Six transparent overlays that react to how excited chat is, not to what it types. Each one measures chat against what is normal for your channel, so a five-person chat and a five-thousand-person chat both get a fair game. Donations, subs, follows and raids count as bigger jolts.

Six ways to spend the excitement

Chat Quake: a live trace along the bottom of the stream. A sudden rush of chat is flagged as a quake, with a magnitude and the word chat was shouting, and the biggest one is kept as the record. Add &style=paper for a paper chart in the corner, or add &theme=night to darken the chart. Inspired by Matthew Busel's ChatQuake.

Quake City: a small skyline along the bottom. Excited chat cracks and topples the buildings one at a time. Flatten it and it is rebuilt a level taller and tougher, so the next knockdown takes more chat.

Hype Flight: chat keeps a little bird in the air through wide, easy gaps. More chat flies higher, the bird ducks under clouds by itself, and only a lull makes it miss.

Chat Volcano: chat builds pressure until it erupts, and the eruption lasts as long as the hype does. The mountain grows after each one.

Hype Rocket: chat fuels a rocket up the side of the screen, from the Cloud Deck to the Moon and beyond. Each destination is farther than the last.

Chat Campfire: every message is a log on the fire. A chat keeping its usual pace holds a steady campfire, excitement builds a bonfire, and silence lets it die down to embers.

Options: &sensitivity=low 또는 high, &scale=1.5, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick, &hud=0 to hide the text, &shake=0 for no shaking, &color=ff8800, 그리고 &align=left, center 또는 right to move the volcano, rocket or campfire. The seismograph also takes &window=120 for the seconds shown and &bg=0 to drop its panel.

Built for the stream, not the page

Portrait games, team towers and show-time scenes

Seven games drawn to be watched at stream size. They show no titles and no instructions: only the game, the numbers that matter, and the one thing to type.

For portrait streams (9:16)

Peg Drop: 입력: !drop to drop your ball from the moving chute. The pegs decide where it lands, and the gold slot pays triple. Chat Blocks: a falling-block puzzle the whole chat steers. Type a column number, 1 대상: 8, to vote where the block lands, or spin to turn it. Sky Drop: 입력: !jump to parachute out of the airship, then left 그리고 right to steer against the wind onto the target. In a 16:9 scene these three keep their portrait shape; add &align=left 또는 right to move them.

Two teams, clear middle

Tower Duel: every chatter is put on one of two teams automatically. Each message drops a crate from that team's crane, and the first tower to reach the flag wins. In a 16:9 scene the towers use only the sides, so a 9:16 video fits between them untouched.

Before, during a break, and after the show

Front Row: a starting-soon countdown. The first message from each chatter drops them into a seat, and the crowd celebrates at zero. Keep It Up: for breaks. Every message bumps the balloon; chat tries to beat its longest rally. Encore: for the end of the show. Every chatter launches a firework, and a busy chat sets off a finale.

Options: &scale=0.8, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. Peg Drop &round=180 (seconds, 0 for endless). Chat Blocks &turn=8 (seconds to vote). Sky Drop &round=75. Tower Duel &goal=12 (crates to win), &gap=0.3 (share of the width left clear), &panel=0 (no backing), &command=!build (only that command drops). Front Row &minutes=5 또는 &until=19:30. Keep It Up &minutes=5 to show a break timer. Encore &crowd=8 (chatters needed for the finale).

Faces first

Races, a boss, a quiz, and a crowd of your chatters

Eleven more pages built around your chatters' own pictures and names. As before: no titles, no instructions, only the game and the one thing to type.

재생

Marble Race: 입력: !play and your marble, wearing your picture, races everyone else down an obstacle course that is different every time. Built for portrait. Cliff Climb: every message is one hop up the cliff, and the first to the flag wins. It uses only the two sides of a 16:9 scene, so a 9:16 video fits in the middle. Stream Boss: every message chips at the boss. Tips, subs and raids hit hard, members hit half again as hard, and whoever lands the final blow becomes the next boss, with their own picture in the frame. The boss heals a little whenever they chat. Trivia: a quiz show. Type a, b, c 또는 d; faster correct answers score more. Ten question packs are built in, or load your own. Stream Bingo: one bingo card for the whole stream, with a picture in every square. When that thing happens, chat types the square's number; enough callers stamp it, and a full line is a bingo.

Always on

Chat Crowd: everyone who chats becomes a little character with their own picture, strolling along the bottom of the stream. They hop when their owner talks, show the first emoji from the message, and obey !jump, !wave, !dance, !hug 그리고 !duel (add a name to pick who).

Before, during a break, and after the show

Roll Call: a starting-soon countdown where everyone who says hello lands on a growing wall of faces. Chat Aquarium: a calm break screen where every chatter is a fish, and a tip drops treasure for the whole tank. Group Photo: an end-of-show class photo. Everyone who says goodbye walks into frame, the camera flashes, and the picture drops out as a print. Winners' Podium: the stream's top players across every game. The games on this page award stars (5, 3 and 2 for the top three of a round, 1 for taking part); the podium adds them up. Chatters type !stars to see their own total. Stars are kept in the browser source's own storage, per session, and start again after twelve quiet hours.

One source, many games

Game Rotation: one browser source that plays a list of games in turn and cross-fades between them. Add &games=marblerace,trivia,pegdrop to choose the list, &every=5 for the minutes each one stays, and &vote to let chat pick the next game by typing 1, 2 또는 3. Every other option on the address is passed on to each game.

Options for every game in this section and the one above: &membersonly (only members and subscribers, and anyone sending a paid message, take part), &scale=0.8, &align=left, &calm (no screen shake or flashes), &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. They slow themselves down on a busy computer rather than slow the stream: first a lower drawing resolution, then fewer frames. &fps=30 또는 &quality=0.75 holds a setting, and &fps=full 는 해당 기능을 끕니다.

Marble Race &lobby=25 (seconds to join), &max=60 (marbles), &command=play. Cliff Climb &goal=20 (hops to the top), &gap=0.3, &command=climb. Stream Boss &hp=1000 (leave it out and the boss grows with the crowd), &align=bottom left. Trivia &pack=general,science (general, science, geography, history, movies, music, gaming, animals, food, sports), &time=20, &rounds=10, &questions=https://... (your own JSON list of [question, right answer, wrong, wrong, wrong], on a host that allows cross-site requests). Stream Bingo &votes=3 (callers needed; a moderator's call counts at once), &lines=1, &squares=Laughs|Pet appears|.... Chat Crowd &max=30, &idle=10 (minutes before a silent character leaves). Roll Call &minutes=5 또는 &until=19:30. Chat Aquarium &minutes=10 (break timer), &idle=10, &water=0 (fish only, over your own break screen). Group Photo &every=45 (seconds between photos), &crowd=12. Winners' Podium &top=8, &season=12 (hours; 0 keeps no scores).

The hype games (Chat Quake, Quake City, Hype Flight, Chat Volcano, Hype Rocket, Chat Campfire) take &skin=winter, spooky, autumn, spring, love 또는 party for a seasonal look. &membersonly also works on the hype games, the bottom-strip games, Quick Call and the card games further down this page. Tug of War, Rock Paper Showdown, Minority Club, Meteor Shield, Beacon Relay, Crew Kitchen, Sum Squad, Memory Parade and Odd One Out are now drawn as scenes with your chatters' pictures in them; their commands and rules are unchanged.

01 / 함께 풀기

Signal Lock(신호 해독)

1?4?

채팅 참여자들이 90초 안에 네 자리 숫자를 맞힙니다. 추측할 때마다 모두에게 단서가 제공됩니다. 퍼즐 팬, 공부 중 휴식, 게임 대기실, 작은 커뮤니티에 어울립니다.

플레이 방법

입력: !code 1234. 숫자는 1~6이며 중복될 수 있습니다. 'Exact'는 숫자와 위치가 모두 맞았다는 뜻입니다. 다른 숫자는 맞는 숫자가 다른 위치에 있는 개수를 나타냅니다.

첫 유효한 추측으로 시간이 시작됩니다. 시청자마다 추측 사이에 5초를 기다려야 하며 같은 추측은 무시됩니다.

02 / 시청자들의 선택에 맡기기

Crowd Quest(시청자 퀘스트)

채팅이 이끄는 다섯 정거장의 우주여행입니다. 경로를 선택하고 공동 보급품을 관리하며 발견한 것을 가지고 돌아오세요. 토크, 그림, 음악, 편안한 게임, 다양한 콘텐츠 방송에 잘 어울립니다.

플레이 방법

입력: !vote 1, !vote 2, 또는 !vote 3. 각 장마다 승무원에게 선택할 시간 30초가 주어집니다. 시청자당 한 표이며, 가장 최근의 유효한 선택으로 이전 투표가 바뀝니다.

첫 투표로 각 장이 시작됩니다. 결과는 투표 전에 표시됩니다. 동률은 무작위로 결정되며, 승무원이 가진 것보다 많은 보급품이 필요한 경로는 선택할 수 없습니다.

Tug of War(줄다리기)

친근한 두 괴물이 코럴 팀과 민트 팀을 위해 줄을 당깁니다. 팀원이 정답을 맞히면 줄이 우리 쪽으로 이동합니다.

미리보기 체험 →

플레이 방법

입력: !join 를 입력하면 인원이 적은 팀에 배정됩니다(같으면 무작위). 양쪽에 한 명씩 있으면 경기가 시작됩니다. 해당 경기 동안 팀은 고정되며 늦게 참가해도 됩니다.

12초마다 단어나 간단한 산수 문제가 나타납니다. 단어나 정답을 채팅에 직접 입력하거나 다음을 사용하세요: !answer 뒤에 답을 입력하세요. 플레이어마다 문제당 한 번 당길 수 있으며 같은 답을 반복해도 추가로 당겨지지 않습니다.

20회 당기기만큼 앞서면 승리합니다. 그렇지 않으면 3분 뒤 앞선 팀이 이기며, 줄이 가운데면 무승부입니다. 결과 화면 이후 시청자들은 다음 경기의 새 팀에 참가합니다. 참여 포인트는 소모되지 않습니다.
