Follows, subs, gifts, tips and cheers

The moments you will see many times a stream. Each one fills in the viewer's name, and the amount or count where there is one.

New follower: heart swarm

Hundreds of tiny glossy hearts fly in from every edge, swirl together into one big beating heart with the new follower's name on a ribbon, then burst and float away. Comes as hearts, stars, thumbs-up, paw prints or snowflakes. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Heart swarm Gold star shower Big thumbs-up Paw prints Snowflake flurry Purple hearts (vertical)

New subscriber: badge forge

A heavy metal emblem slams into place, still glowing from the forge, with a flash and a shower of sparks. A shine runs across it, laurel branches open either side and the subscriber's name is on a plaque underneath; a small medal shows the months when it is more than one. Shield, star, crown or hexagon, in six metals. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Gold shield Silver star Rose gold crown Emerald hexagon Obsidian shield, red glow Amethyst crown (vertical)

Gifted subs: parachute gifts

Wrapped presents float down on little parachutes, one for every gift, land with a bounce and pop open in a puff of confetti and light. The gifter's name and how many they gave hang on a gift tag. Five wrapping styles. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Classic presents Festive red and green Neon drop Pastel party Gold foil Classic presents (vertical)

Tip: treasure chest

A treasure chest slams down, rattles, then bursts open with a beam of light and a fountain of spinning coins that pile up around it. The amount rides a ribbon above the chest and the tipper's name is on the plate below; at the end the chest swallows its treasure again and leaps away. Coins, gems, banknotes or hearts, from four kinds of chest. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Pirate chest of gold Royal chest of gems Dark iron hoard Sci-fi cash crate Chest full of hearts Pirate chest (vertical)

Cheers: gem cascade

Cut gems rain down and shatter into coloured shards and flashes of light on a line of glass, while a counter rolls up to the amount cheered. The viewer's name sits underneath. The gems change colour with the size of the cheer: grey, purple, green, blue, red, then rainbow. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Purple gem cascade Grey sprinkle Green thousand Blue five thousand Red ten thousand Rainbow jackpot (vertical)

Small thank-yous, raids and milestones

Lighter touches for likes and shares, a sign for your regulars, a fleet for a raid and a toast for your supporters.

Likes & shares: sticker pop

A small glossy sticker slaps into a corner with a bounce and a ring of sparks, the viewer's name on a tab under it, then peels off and flutters away. Made for the little things that happen often: a like, a share, a first message. Eight stickers (heart, thumbs-up, share arrow, star, speech bubble, paw, flame, waving hand), six places to put it. Quick, light and fully transparent again in about four seconds.

All styles
Heart for a like Thumbs-up, bottom left Share arrow, top middle First message bubble Paw print, bottom right Wave hello (vertical)

Neon thank-you sign

A neon sign swings down on two chains with the viewer's name written in glowing tubes. The tubes flicker on one by one, a couple misfire and spit sparks, then it burns steadily, goes dark tube by tube and is hauled back up. Five colour pairs, five shapes beside the name and five backboards. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Pink and cyan heart Warm white on dark brick Green and yellow on wood Purple script on a clear sheet Ice blue on steel mesh Purple and orange (vertical)

Raid: the fleet arrives

A fleet sweeps in from the left in formation, ribbons streaming, and the lead craft tows a long banner with the raiding channel's name while a counter ticks up to the number of raiders. Then it runs off, comes back once the other way further off, and is gone. Paper planes, airships, rockets, pirate galleons on a strip of sea or little UFOs. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Paper planes Airships, gold and red Rocket ships Pirate galleons Little UFOs Blue paper planes (vertical)

Level up: pillar of light

A magic circle draws itself on the floor, a pillar of light slams down into it, rings of light climb the pillar and the viewer's name rises in bright metal with a small caption above. A badge shows a number when you give it one (a level, months, a streak) and ticks up to it. For milestones, resubs and loyalty streaks. Six looks, from arcane gold to chunky pixels. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Arcane gold Frost, three months Crimson rank up Cosmic violet Pixel level up Emerald streak (vertical)

A toast: glasses clink

Two glasses swing in from either side and clink in the middle with a ring of light and a spray of drops; the drink sloshes, bubbles or steam keep rising, and the viewer's name unrolls on a thin ribbon underneath. Then the glasses part. Champagne flutes, coffee mugs, tea cups, soda with ice or frothy mugs. For members and top supporters. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Champagne flutes Coffee mugs Tea cups Soda with ice Frothy mugs Pink champagne (vertical)

Huge donations

Takeovers for the few moments a month that deserve the whole screen. Each has a gentle setting, and an off setting, for shake and flashes.

Mega moment: meteor strike

For the biggest donation of the night. A point of light swells in the sky, then a burning meteor tears down across the screen and hits: a flash, a shock ring, flying debris and a glowing crater along the bottom. The amount rises out of the impact white-hot and cools into solid metal, with the viewer's name beneath. Fire, ice comet, emerald, gold or void. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Fire meteor Ice comet Emerald meteor Golden meteor Void meteor Fire meteor (vertical)

Mega moment: dragon flyby

A huge dragon crosses the sky, turns, and on its second pass breathes a river of fire across the screen. The fire burns the amount and the viewer's name into view; they glow, then char and crumble into sparks. Red fire dragon, frost dragon, jade serpent, shadow dragon or golden dragon. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Red fire dragon Frost dragon Jade serpent Shadow dragon Golden dragon Red dragon (vertical)

Mega moment: the vault opens

A massive round vault door fills the screen. The wheel spins, the bolts pull back one by one, and the door swings open: light floods out and gold bars, coins and banknotes pour out into a heap while the amount counts up on a display, with the viewer's name on a plaque. Steel, gold, bronze, sci-fi or ice. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Steel bank vault Golden vault Dark bronze vault Sci-fi vault Ice vault Steel vault (vertical)

Mega moment: rocket launch

A rocket with the viewer's name on its side stands on the pad. The countdown lamps light one by one, the engines catch, smoke rolls out along the ground and it climbs out of the top of the screen. Then a huge firework bursts with the amount inside it and the name beneath, while more shells go off around it. Classic white, retro red and white, sci-fi, toy or golden. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Classic rocket launch Retro rocket Sci-fi launch Toy rocket Golden rocket Classic rocket (vertical)

Mega moment: lightning storm

Dark clouds roll in along the top, rain comes down and forks of lightning stab at the ground, lighting the clouds from inside. Then one giant bolt hits the middle of the screen and leaves the viewer's name there in crackling electric letters, with the amount beneath. White-blue, violet, toxic green, red or gold. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
White-blue lightning storm Violet storm Toxic green storm Red storm Golden storm Lightning storm (vertical)

Mega moment: supernova

A star swells in the middle of the screen, falls in on itself, and after a beat of darkness goes off: a blinding flash, a racing shock ring, a slower ring of glowing gas and hundreds of stars thrown outward. The amount appears in the afterglow with the viewer's name beneath while the gas drifts and fades. Five colours and three shapes of cloud. Clears to fully transparent when it ends.

All styles
Blue-white supernova Magenta nebula Golden starburst Teal supernova Rainbow starburst Blue supernova (vertical)
