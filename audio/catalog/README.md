# Original SSN sounds

Browse and listen in [Sound Library](../../audio-library.html). In Event Flow, choose **Play Audio Clip → Browse sounds**. In Multi-Alerts settings, use **Browse sounds** beside the sound you want to change. Previews play locally; live Event Flow audio plays through the Flow Actions browser source.

The original sound files in this directory are offered under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). You may use, modify, and redistribute them, including in monetized streams. Attribution is optional.

Effects use original procedural synthesis without imported sound samples. Spoken phrases are synthetic recordings generated ahead of time with [Kokoro-82M v1.0](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), using its Apache-2.0-licensed model and English, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese voices. Playing these MP3 files does not load a speech model or call a speech service. The model's license remains separate from the audio files.
