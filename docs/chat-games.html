Keep an eye on your name

Quick Call

Three names. Fresh four-character codes. Be ready when it is your turn.

Try the preview →

How to play

Type !quick to join. When called, type your own code within 30 seconds. Correct replies rotate back into the queue; missed turns require joining again. The overlay ranks the five fastest personal-best times. Codes ignore capitalization.

Transparent OBS background. Add &clean to hide host controls. Pause freezes the game; Clear scores or reloading starts fresh. Scores stay only in this page. No bot messages, points, or purchases. Times include stream and chat delay, so this is a casual competition. Random codes discourage prepared answers; they cannot prevent automated reading.

Made for OBS layers

Play over your stream

Firefly Catch and Comet Rally leave the surrounding page transparent; Maze Raid fills its source. Start with a 1280 × 800 browser source. Add &clean to hide host buttons and connection status. Preview pages show a checkerboard to make the transparent areas visible.

Three new ways to play

Firefly Catch: type !catch to start, then catch when the firefly enters the ring. One attempt per viewer per flight; a centered catch earns 3 points, other catches in the ring earn 1. Six flights, then a winner.

Comet Rally: type !boost to join an automatically assigned team. Each viewer can boost once every 3 seconds. First to 20 boosts wins, or the leader after 60 seconds.

Maze Raid: a faster auto-roaming explorer navigates textured brick-and-metal corridors with SSN wall signs. Ordinary chat spawns one avatar enemy per chatter, up to 40 per round. Raiders spawn 3 to 8 corridor steps away, chase and attack; the final hit wins. Failed or missing avatar images show initials. The maze fills its OBS browser source; resize the source to fit your scene.

All three restart after an 8-second result screen. Host pause and New round are available through OBS Interact. No points are spent and no bot messages are sent.

Signal Lock

1?4?

Chat has 90 seconds to crack four digits. Every guess gives everyone a clue. Great for puzzle fans, study breaks, gaming lobbies, and smaller communities.

How to play

Type !code 1234. Digits range from 1 to 6, and can repeat. “Exact” means the correct digit and position. The other count shows correct digits in different positions.

The first valid guess starts the clock. Five seconds between each viewer’s guesses; identical guesses are ignored.

Crowd Quest

A five-stop space trip guided by chat. Choose routes, manage shared supplies, and bring home discoveries. A relaxed fit for talk, art, music, cozy gaming, and variety streams.

How to play

Type !vote 1, !vote 2, or !vote 3. Each chapter gives the crew 30 seconds to choose. One vote per viewer; the latest valid choice replaces their previous vote.

The first vote starts each chapter. Outcomes are shown before voting. Ties are random, and routes needing more supplies than the crew has are unavailable.

Two teams / One rope

Tug of War

Two friendly monsters pull for Team Coral and Team Mint. Correct answers from your teammates move the rope toward your side.

Try the preview →

How to play

Type !join to get assigned to the smaller team (random when tied). A match starts once both sides have a player. Teams stay fixed for that match; late joiners are welcome.

A word or simple arithmetic question appears every 12 seconds. Type the word or answer directly in chat, or use !answer followed by your answer. Each player can earn one tug per prompt; repeated answers do not add more tugs.

A lead of 20 tugs wins. Otherwise, the side ahead after three minutes wins; an even rope is a draw. After the result screen, viewers join fresh teams for the next match. No loyalty points are spent.

Put it on stream

  1. In SSN, open Interactive Games → Select Game.
  2. Choose one of the games above and add its session link as an OBS Browser Source. Start at 1280 × 900.
  3. Keep SSN and your chat sources running. Show the on-screen command and let chat start the round.

The host can pause or restart using OBS’s Interact window. Add &clean to hide host buttons, &transparent for a transparent page background, or &chroma for green. Add &demo only for an isolated preview. A round ends with a ten-second result screen, then automatically moves on.

These games use no loyalty points, purchases, external media, or viewer login page. They only read the chosen SSN chat session; they do not send commands back to chat. Host buttons act locally. The same person on two platforms counts as two players. Up to 1,000 distinct players per round.
