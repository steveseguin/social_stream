Recover settings from your links

Paste your Social Stream links to create one settings file. Combine a dock, featured chat, alerts, and other overlays from the same session.

Only settings contained in these links can be recovered. Links are read in your browser; they are not opened or submitted.

Pasting just a query string?

For example: ?session=YOUR_ID&color. Full links are detected automatically.

    In the Social Stream menu, use Import settings to open the downloaded .data file. Importing replaces your saved settings; export your current settings first if you want to keep them.
