Chamada rápida (Quick Call)

Três nomes. Novos códigos de quatro caracteres. Esteja pronto quando chegar sua vez.

Experimente a prévia →

Como jogar

Digite !quick para entrar. Quando chamado, digite seu próprio código em até 30 segundos. Respostas corretas voltam para a fila; perder a vez exige entrar novamente. A sobreposição classifica os cinco menores recordes pessoais. Os códigos não diferenciam maiúsculas de minúsculas.

Transparent OBS background. Refreshing the browser source starts fresh. Scores stay only in this page. No bot messages, points, or purchases. Times include stream and chat delay, so this is a casual competition. Random codes discourage prepared answers; they cannot prevent automated reading.

Feito para camadas no OBS

Jogue sobre sua transmissão

Firefly Catch and Comet Rally leave the surrounding page transparent; Maze Raid fills its source. Start with a 1280 × 800 browser source. Preview pages show a checkerboard to make the transparent areas visible.

Três novas formas de jogar

Caça ao vaga-lume (Firefly Catch): digite !catch para começar e capture quando o vaga-lume entrar no anel. Uma tentativa por espectador por voo; uma captura centralizada vale 3 pontos, outras dentro do anel valem 1. Seis voos, depois um vencedor.

Corrida de cometas (Comet Rally): digite !boost para entrar em um time atribuído automaticamente. Cada espectador pode impulsionar uma vez a cada 3 segundos. Vence quem chegar primeiro a 20 impulsos, ou quem liderar após 60 segundos.

Invasão ao labirinto (Maze Raid): um explorador mais rápido que se move automaticamente percorre corredores texturizados de tijolo e metal com placas do SSN. O chat normal gera um inimigo com avatar por participante, até 40 por rodada. Invasores surgem a 3–8 passos de corredor, perseguem e atacam; o golpe final vence. Imagens de avatar ausentes ou com falha mostram iniciais. O labirinto preenche sua Fonte de navegador do OBS; redimensione a fonte para caber na cena.

All three restart after an 8-second result screen. No points are spent and no bot messages are sent.

Games that run on hype

Six transparent overlays that react to how excited chat is, not to what it types. Each one measures chat against what is normal for your channel, so a five-person chat and a five-thousand-person chat both get a fair game. Donations, subs, follows and raids count as bigger jolts.

Six ways to spend the excitement

Chat Quake: a live trace along the bottom of the stream. A sudden rush of chat is flagged as a quake, with a magnitude and the word chat was shouting, and the biggest one is kept as the record. Add &style=paper for a paper chart in the corner, or add &theme=night to darken the chart. Inspired by Matthew Busel's ChatQuake.

Quake City: a small skyline along the bottom. Excited chat cracks and topples the buildings one at a time. Flatten it and it is rebuilt a level taller and tougher, so the next knockdown takes more chat.

Hype Flight: chat keeps a little bird in the air through wide, easy gaps. More chat flies higher, the bird ducks under clouds by itself, and only a lull makes it miss.

Chat Volcano: chat builds pressure until it erupts, and the eruption lasts as long as the hype does. The mountain grows after each one.

Hype Rocket: chat fuels a rocket up the side of the screen, from the Cloud Deck to the Moon and beyond. Each destination is farther than the last.

Chat Campfire: every message is a log on the fire. A chat keeping its usual pace holds a steady campfire, excitement builds a bonfire, and silence lets it die down to embers.

Options: &sensitivity=low ou high, &scale=1.5, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick, &hud=0 to hide the text, &shake=0 for no shaking, &color=ff8800, e &align=left, center ou right to move the volcano, rocket or campfire. The seismograph also takes &window=120 for the seconds shown and &bg=0 to drop its panel.

Built for the stream, not the page

Portrait games, team towers and show-time scenes

Seven games drawn to be watched at stream size. They show no titles and no instructions: only the game, the numbers that matter, and the one thing to type.

For portrait streams (9:16)

Peg Drop: digite !drop to drop your ball from the moving chute. The pegs decide where it lands, and the gold slot pays triple. Chat Blocks: a falling-block puzzle the whole chat steers. Type a column number, 1 como 8, to vote where the block lands, or spin to turn it. Sky Drop: digite !jump to parachute out of the airship, then left e right to steer against the wind onto the target. In a 16:9 scene these three keep their portrait shape; add &align=left ou right to move them.

Two teams, clear middle

Tower Duel: every chatter is put on one of two teams automatically. Each message drops a crate from that team's crane, and the first tower to reach the flag wins. In a 16:9 scene the towers use only the sides, so a 9:16 video fits between them untouched.

Before, during a break, and after the show

Front Row: a starting-soon countdown. The first message from each chatter drops them into a seat, and the crowd celebrates at zero. Keep It Up: for breaks. Every message bumps the balloon; chat tries to beat its longest rally. Encore: for the end of the show. Every chatter launches a firework, and a busy chat sets off a finale.

Options: &scale=0.8, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. Peg Drop &round=180 (seconds, 0 for endless). Chat Blocks &turn=8 (seconds to vote). Sky Drop &round=75. Tower Duel &goal=12 (crates to win), &gap=0.3 (share of the width left clear), &panel=0 (no backing), &command=!build (only that command drops). Front Row &minutes=5 ou &until=19:30. Keep It Up &minutes=5 to show a break timer. Encore &crowd=8 (chatters needed for the finale).

Faces first

Races, a boss, a quiz, and a crowd of your chatters

Eleven more pages built around your chatters' own pictures and names. As before: no titles, no instructions, only the game and the one thing to type.

Reproduzir

Marble Race: digite !play and your marble, wearing your picture, races everyone else down an obstacle course that is different every time. Built for portrait. Cliff Climb: every message is one hop up the cliff, and the first to the flag wins. It uses only the two sides of a 16:9 scene, so a 9:16 video fits in the middle. Stream Boss: every message chips at the boss. Tips, subs and raids hit hard, members hit half again as hard, and whoever lands the final blow becomes the next boss, with their own picture in the frame. The boss heals a little whenever they chat. Trivia: a quiz show. Type a, b, c ou d; faster correct answers score more. Ten question packs are built in, or load your own. Stream Bingo: one bingo card for the whole stream, with a picture in every square. When that thing happens, chat types the square's number; enough callers stamp it, and a full line is a bingo.

Always on

Chat Crowd: everyone who chats becomes a little character with their own picture, strolling along the bottom of the stream. They hop when their owner talks, show the first emoji from the message, and obey !jump, !wave, !dance, !hug e !duel (add a name to pick who).

Before, during a break, and after the show

Roll Call: a starting-soon countdown where everyone who says hello lands on a growing wall of faces. Chat Aquarium: a calm break screen where every chatter is a fish, and a tip drops treasure for the whole tank. Group Photo: an end-of-show class photo. Everyone who says goodbye walks into frame, the camera flashes, and the picture drops out as a print. Winners' Podium: the stream's top players across every game. The games on this page award stars (5, 3 and 2 for the top three of a round, 1 for taking part); the podium adds them up. Chatters type !stars to see their own total. Stars are kept in the browser source's own storage, per session, and start again after twelve quiet hours.

One source, many games

Game Rotation: one browser source that plays a list of games in turn and cross-fades between them. Add &games=marblerace,trivia,pegdrop to choose the list, &every=5 for the minutes each one stays, and &vote to let chat pick the next game by typing 1, 2 ou 3. Every other option on the address is passed on to each game.

Options for every game in this section and the one above: &membersonly (only members and subscribers, and anyone sending a paid message, take part), &scale=0.8, &align=left, &calm (no screen shake or flashes), &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. They slow themselves down on a busy computer rather than slow the stream: first a lower drawing resolution, then fewer frames. &fps=30 ou &quality=0.75 holds a setting, and &fps=full desativa esse comportamento.

Marble Race &lobby=25 (seconds to join), &max=60 (marbles), &command=play. Cliff Climb &goal=20 (hops to the top), &gap=0.3, &command=climb. Stream Boss &hp=1000 (leave it out and the boss grows with the crowd), &align=bottom left. Trivia &pack=general,science (general, science, geography, history, movies, music, gaming, animals, food, sports), &time=20, &rounds=10, &questions=https://... (your own JSON list of [question, right answer, wrong, wrong, wrong], on a host that allows cross-site requests). Stream Bingo &votes=3 (callers needed; a moderator's call counts at once), &lines=1, &squares=Laughs|Pet appears|.... Chat Crowd &max=30, &idle=10 (minutes before a silent character leaves). Roll Call &minutes=5 ou &until=19:30. Chat Aquarium &minutes=10 (break timer), &idle=10, &water=0 (fish only, over your own break screen). Group Photo &every=45 (seconds between photos), &crowd=12. Winners' Podium &top=8, &season=12 (hours; 0 keeps no scores).

The hype games (Chat Quake, Quake City, Hype Flight, Chat Volcano, Hype Rocket, Chat Campfire) take &skin=winter, spooky, autumn, spring, love ou party for a seasonal look. &membersonly also works on the hype games, the bottom-strip games, Quick Call and the card games further down this page. Tug of War, Rock Paper Showdown, Minority Club, Meteor Shield, Beacon Relay, Crew Kitchen, Sum Squad, Memory Parade and Odd One Out are now drawn as scenes with your chatters' pictures in them; their commands and rules are unchanged.

1?4?

O chat tem 90 segundos para descobrir quatro dígitos. Cada palpite dá uma pista a todos. Ótimo para fãs de enigmas, pausas de estudo, salas de jogos e comunidades menores.

Como jogar

Digite !code 1234. Os dígitos vão de 1 a 6 e podem se repetir. “Exato” significa dígito e posição corretos. A outra contagem mostra dígitos corretos em posições diferentes.

O primeiro palpite válido inicia o relógio. Cinco segundos entre os palpites de cada espectador; palpites idênticos são ignorados.

Missão do público (Crowd Quest)

Uma viagem espacial de cinco paradas guiada pelo chat. Escolham rotas, gerenciem suprimentos compartilhados e tragam descobertas para casa. Uma opção tranquila para transmissões de conversa, arte, música, jogos aconchegantes e variedades.

Como jogar

Digite !vote 1, !vote 2, ou !vote 3. Cada capítulo dá 30 segundos para a tripulação escolher. Um voto por espectador; a escolha válida mais recente substitui o voto anterior.

O primeiro voto inicia cada capítulo. Os resultados são mostrados antes de votar. Empates são aleatórios e rotas que exigem mais suprimentos do que a tripulação tem ficam indisponíveis.

Cabo de guerra (Tug of War)

Dois monstros amigáveis puxam pelo Time Coral e pelo Time Menta. Respostas corretas dos seus colegas movem a corda para o seu lado.

Experimente a prévia →

Como jogar

Digite !join para entrar no menor time (aleatório em caso de empate). A partida começa quando ambos os lados têm um jogador. Os times ficam fixos nessa partida; novos participantes são bem-vindos.

Uma palavra ou pergunta simples de matemática aparece a cada 12 segundos. Digite a palavra ou resposta diretamente no chat, ou use !answer seguido da resposta. Cada jogador pode ganhar um puxão por pergunta; respostas repetidas não somam mais puxões.

Uma vantagem de 20 puxões vence. Caso contrário, vence o lado à frente após três minutos; corda equilibrada é empate. Após a tela de resultado, os espectadores entram em novos times para a próxima partida. Nenhum ponto de fidelidade é gasto.

Escudo de meteoros (Meteor Shield)

Proteja a nave durante seis ondas de meteoros. Combine os escudos com as cores que chegam.

Experimente a prévia →

Como jogar

Digite !shield red.

Um escudo por espectador por onda. Mude de faixa uma vez por segundo. Cada escudo bloqueia um meteoro da mesma cor; cada meteoro não bloqueado tira um ponto do casco. As ondas se adaptam ao tamanho da tripulação, até 12 meteoros.

Como jogar

Um pouco de soma. Muito trabalho em equipe.

Equipe da soma (Sum Squad)

Trabalhem juntos para chegar exatamente a 20, depois 30 e depois 40.

Experimente a prévia →

Como jogar

Digite !add 1 até !add 9. Espectadores diferentes se alternam em cada alvo; cada pessoa pode somar uma vez a cada 3 segundos. Valores que ultrapassam o alvo são ignorados. Alcancem os três alvos em 90 segundos.

Progresso e pontuações reiniciam a cada partida. Nenhum ponto é gasto.

Coloque na transmissão

  1. No SSN, abra Jogos interativos → Selecionar jogo.
  2. Escolha um dos jogos acima e adicione seu link de sessão como Fonte de navegador do OBS. Comece com 1280 × 900.
  3. Mantenha o SSN e as fontes de chat em execução. Mostre o comando na tela e deixe o chat começar a rodada.

Rounds advance automatically. To restart a game, refresh its browser source. Add &transparent para um fundo de página transparente, ou &chroma para verde. Adicione &demo only for an isolated preview.

These games use no loyalty points, purchases, external media, or viewer login page. They only read the chosen SSN chat session; they do not send commands back to chat. The same person on two platforms counts as two players. Up to 1,000 distinct players per round.

Para Pulso rítmico, set Volume in SSN's Game Options before copying the link. You can also add &volume=0 por 100; 0 mutes audio and the default is 50.
