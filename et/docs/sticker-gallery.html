Host setup

You choose what
gets on screen.

These rewards use SSN’s own loyalty balance, not native Twitch or Kick channel points. Enable them only when you want this interaction.

Default limits: 6 seconds on screen, 60 seconds per viewer, 20 seconds per sticker, and a 3-second gap. Requests during a cooldown are declined without a charge.

  1. Avatud Points System → Sticker rewards in the SSN controls. Enable the points system and sticker rewards.
  2. Choose one or more packs and your prices. Set cooldowns and duration for your show.
  3. Lisa genereeritud Sticker overlay session link as an OBS browser source. Keep it active while accepting redemptions.
  4. Alternatively, add &stickers to your Multi-Alerts or event-stream URL. Use one sticker display to avoid showing the reward twice.
  5. Tell viewers to use !stickers for the enabled menu and !sticker rocket to redeem. Source-chat replies are optional and require a supported send-back connection.

The sticker connection uses the standard SSN iframe bridge, including when your chat uses server mode. A local-only deployment needs access to that bridge.

Bring your own personality

Your mascot.
Your favorite GIF.

Add up to 12 custom rewards from the same settings panel. Choose a name, a short code, a fixed price, and a direct HTTPS image URL. Viewers choose from your approved menu; they cannot submit arbitrary image URLs.

Using a GIPHY reaction

Copy a direct GIF or WebP media link, rather than the GIPHY page address, and add it as a custom sticker. The image is fetched from its host when used; that host must permit image loading.

A custom code of hype becomes !sticker custom-hype. Static PNG, JPEG, and WebP images work too. Use artwork you have permission to display.

For a calm layout, add &motion=off to stop the built-in sticker movement. Animated GIF content still animates, so choose static images when you want no motion. Optional &size=400 ja &hidecaption adjust the player.
