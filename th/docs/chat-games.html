No commands needed

Games that run on hype

Six transparent overlays that react to how excited chat is, not to what it types. Each one measures chat against what is normal for your channel, so a five-person chat and a five-thousand-person chat both get a fair game. Donations, subs, follows and raids count as bigger jolts.

Six ways to spend the excitement

Chat Quake: a live trace along the bottom of the stream. A sudden rush of chat is flagged as a quake, with a magnitude and the word chat was shouting, and the biggest one is kept as the record. Add &style=paper for a paper chart in the corner, or add &theme=night to darken the chart. Inspired by Matthew Busel's ChatQuake.

Quake City: a small skyline along the bottom. Excited chat cracks and topples the buildings one at a time. Flatten it and it is rebuilt a level taller and tougher, so the next knockdown takes more chat.

Hype Flight: chat keeps a little bird in the air through wide, easy gaps. More chat flies higher, the bird ducks under clouds by itself, and only a lull makes it miss.

Chat Volcano: chat builds pressure until it erupts, and the eruption lasts as long as the hype does. The mountain grows after each one.

Hype Rocket: chat fuels a rocket up the side of the screen, from the Cloud Deck to the Moon and beyond. Each destination is farther than the last.

Chat Campfire: every message is a log on the fire. A chat keeping its usual pace holds a steady campfire, excitement builds a bonfire, and silence lets it die down to embers.

Options: &sensitivity=low หรือ high, &scale=1.5, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick, &hud=0 to hide the text, &shake=0 for no shaking, &color=ff8800, และ &align=left, center หรือ right to move the volcano, rocket or campfire. The seismograph also takes &window=120 for the seconds shown and &bg=0 to drop its panel.

Built for the stream, not the page

Portrait games, team towers and show-time scenes

Seven games drawn to be watched at stream size. They show no titles and no instructions: only the game, the numbers that matter, and the one thing to type.

For portrait streams (9:16)

Peg Drop: พิมพ์ !drop to drop your ball from the moving chute. The pegs decide where it lands, and the gold slot pays triple. Chat Blocks: a falling-block puzzle the whole chat steers. Type a column number, 1 เป็น 8, to vote where the block lands, or spin to turn it. Sky Drop: พิมพ์ !jump to parachute out of the airship, then left และ right to steer against the wind onto the target. In a 16:9 scene these three keep their portrait shape; add &align=left หรือ right to move them.

Two teams, clear middle

Tower Duel: every chatter is put on one of two teams automatically. Each message drops a crate from that team's crane, and the first tower to reach the flag wins. In a 16:9 scene the towers use only the sides, so a 9:16 video fits between them untouched.

Before, during a break, and after the show

Front Row: a starting-soon countdown. The first message from each chatter drops them into a seat, and the crowd celebrates at zero. Keep It Up: for breaks. Every message bumps the balloon; chat tries to beat its longest rally. Encore: for the end of the show. Every chatter launches a firework, and a busy chat sets off a finale.

Options: &scale=0.8, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. Peg Drop &round=180 (seconds, 0 for endless). Chat Blocks &turn=8 (seconds to vote). Sky Drop &round=75. Tower Duel &goal=12 (crates to win), &gap=0.3 (share of the width left clear), &panel=0 (no backing), &command=!build (only that command drops). Front Row &minutes=5 หรือ &until=19:30. Keep It Up &minutes=5 to show a break timer. Encore &crowd=8 (chatters needed for the finale).

Faces first

Races, a boss, a quiz, and a crowd of your chatters

Eleven more pages built around your chatters' own pictures and names. As before: no titles, no instructions, only the game and the one thing to type.

เล่น

Marble Race: พิมพ์ !play and your marble, wearing your picture, races everyone else down an obstacle course that is different every time. Built for portrait. Cliff Climb: every message is one hop up the cliff, and the first to the flag wins. It uses only the two sides of a 16:9 scene, so a 9:16 video fits in the middle. Stream Boss: every message chips at the boss. Tips, subs and raids hit hard, members hit half again as hard, and whoever lands the final blow becomes the next boss, with their own picture in the frame. The boss heals a little whenever they chat. Trivia: a quiz show. Type a, b, c หรือ d; faster correct answers score more. Ten question packs are built in, or load your own. Stream Bingo: one bingo card for the whole stream, with a picture in every square. When that thing happens, chat types the square's number; enough callers stamp it, and a full line is a bingo.

Always on

Chat Crowd: everyone who chats becomes a little character with their own picture, strolling along the bottom of the stream. They hop when their owner talks, show the first emoji from the message, and obey !jump, !wave, !dance, !hug และ !duel (add a name to pick who).

Before, during a break, and after the show

Roll Call: a starting-soon countdown where everyone who says hello lands on a growing wall of faces. Chat Aquarium: a calm break screen where every chatter is a fish, and a tip drops treasure for the whole tank. Group Photo: an end-of-show class photo. Everyone who says goodbye walks into frame, the camera flashes, and the picture drops out as a print. Winners' Podium: the stream's top players across every game. The games on this page award stars (5, 3 and 2 for the top three of a round, 1 for taking part); the podium adds them up. Chatters type !stars to see their own total. Stars are kept in the browser source's own storage, per session, and start again after twelve quiet hours.

One source, many games

Game Rotation: one browser source that plays a list of games in turn and cross-fades between them. Add &games=marblerace,trivia,pegdrop to choose the list, &every=5 for the minutes each one stays, and &vote to let chat pick the next game by typing 1, 2 หรือ 3. Every other option on the address is passed on to each game.

Options for every game in this section and the one above: &membersonly (only members and subscribers, and anyone sending a paid message, take part), &scale=0.8, &align=left, &calm (no screen shake or flashes), &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. They slow themselves down on a busy computer rather than slow the stream: first a lower drawing resolution, then fewer frames. &fps=30 หรือ &quality=0.75 holds a setting, and &fps=full ปิดฟีเจอร์นั้น

Marble Race &lobby=25 (seconds to join), &max=60 (marbles), &command=play. Cliff Climb &goal=20 (hops to the top), &gap=0.3, &command=climb. Stream Boss &hp=1000 (leave it out and the boss grows with the crowd), &align=bottom left. Trivia &pack=general,science (general, science, geography, history, movies, music, gaming, animals, food, sports), &time=20, &rounds=10, &questions=https://... (your own JSON list of [question, right answer, wrong, wrong, wrong], on a host that allows cross-site requests). Stream Bingo &votes=3 (callers needed; a moderator's call counts at once), &lines=1, &squares=Laughs|Pet appears|.... Chat Crowd &max=30, &idle=10 (minutes before a silent character leaves). Roll Call &minutes=5 หรือ &until=19:30. Chat Aquarium &minutes=10 (break timer), &idle=10, &water=0 (fish only, over your own break screen). Group Photo &every=45 (seconds between photos), &crowd=12. Winners' Podium &top=8, &season=12 (hours; 0 keeps no scores).

The hype games (Chat Quake, Quake City, Hype Flight, Chat Volcano, Hype Rocket, Chat Campfire) take &skin=winter, spooky, autumn, spring, love หรือ party for a seasonal look. &membersonly also works on the hype games, the bottom-strip games, Quick Call and the card games further down this page. นำขึ้นสตรีม

  1. ใน SSN เปิด เกมแบบโต้ตอบ (Interactive Games) → เลือกเกม (Select Game).
  2. เลือกเกมด้านบนแล้วเพิ่มลิงก์เซสชันเป็นแหล่งเบราว์เซอร์ OBS เริ่มที่ 1280 × 900
  3. เปิด SSN และแหล่งแชตทิ้งไว้ แสดงคำสั่งบนหน้าจอแล้วให้แชตเริ่มรอบ

Rounds advance automatically. To restart a game, refresh its browser source. Add &transparent เพื่อให้พื้นหลังหน้าโปร่งใส หรือ &chroma สำหรับสีเขียว เพิ่ม &demo only for an isolated preview.

These games use no loyalty points, purchases, external media, or viewer login page. They only read the chosen SSN chat session; they do not send commands back to chat. The same person on two platforms counts as two players. Up to 1,000 distinct players per round.

สำหรับ Rhythm Pulse, set Volume in SSN's Game Options before copying the link. You can also add &volume=0 ผ่าน 100; 0 mutes audio and the default is 50.

Host a phrase game

ใน SSN เปิด Interactive Games → Phrase Guess host tool. Enter your phrases and select Start Game. Keep this page private; use Dock to show prompts and results to viewers. Try the host preview.
