How comments are captured

The first complete check shows existing comments here as history. Only new comment IDs found on later checks are forwarded to Social Stream.

Replies, edits and deletion updates are not captured. Comments received while Social Stream is off or unavailable stay in this preview and are not replayed later. Reconnecting starts a new history snapshot.
