Nasıl oynanır?

Yazın: !quick ile katılın. Çağrıldığınızda kendi kodunuzu 30 saniye içinde yazın. Doğru yanıt verenler kuyruğa geri döner; sırasını kaçıranların yeniden katılması gerekir. Katman, kişisel en iyi süreler arasından en hızlı beşini sıralar. Kodlarda büyük/küçük harf ayrımı yoktur.

Transparent OBS background. Refreshing the browser source starts fresh. Scores stay only in this page. No bot messages, points, or purchases. Times include stream and chat delay, so this is a casual competition. Random codes discourage prepared answers; they cannot prevent automated reading.

Yayınınızın üzerinde oynayın

Üç yeni oynama şekli

Ateşböceği yakalama: yazın: !catch ile başlayın, ardından ateşböceği halkanın içine girdiğinde yakalayın. Her uçuşta izleyici başına bir deneme; merkezde yakalama 3 puan, halka içindeki diğer yakalamalar 1 puan kazandırır. Altı uçuştan sonra kazanan belirlenir.

Kuyrukluyıldız yarışı: yazın: !boost ile otomatik atanan bir takıma katılın. Her izleyici 3 saniyede bir hızlandırabilir. 20 hızlandırmaya ilk ulaşan veya 60 saniye sonunda önde olan kazanır.

Labirent baskını: daha hızlı ve kendiliğinden dolaşan bir kâşif, SSN tabelalarının bulunduğu dokulu tuğla ve metal koridorlarda ilerler. Normal sohbet, her katılımcı için bir avatar düşman oluşturur; tur başına en fazla 40 düşman olabilir. Baskıncılar koridorda 3 ila 8 adım uzakta belirir, kovalar ve saldırır; son darbeyi vuran kazanır. Yüklenemeyen veya eksik avatar resimleri yerine baş harfler gösterilir. Labirent OBS tarayıcı kaynağını doldurur; kaynağı sahnenize uyacak şekilde boyutlandırın.

All three restart after an 8-second result screen. No points are spent and no bot messages are sent.

No commands needed

Games that run on hype

Six transparent overlays that react to how excited chat is, not to what it types. Each one measures chat against what is normal for your channel, so a five-person chat and a five-thousand-person chat both get a fair game. Donations, subs, follows and raids count as bigger jolts.

Six ways to spend the excitement

Chat Quake: a live trace along the bottom of the stream. A sudden rush of chat is flagged as a quake, with a magnitude and the word chat was shouting, and the biggest one is kept as the record. Add &style=paper for a paper chart in the corner, or add &theme=night to darken the chart. Inspired by Matthew Busel's ChatQuake.

Quake City: a small skyline along the bottom. Excited chat cracks and topples the buildings one at a time. Flatten it and it is rebuilt a level taller and tougher, so the next knockdown takes more chat.

Hype Flight: chat keeps a little bird in the air through wide, easy gaps. More chat flies higher, the bird ducks under clouds by itself, and only a lull makes it miss.

Chat Volcano: chat builds pressure until it erupts, and the eruption lasts as long as the hype does. The mountain grows after each one.

Hype Rocket: chat fuels a rocket up the side of the screen, from the Cloud Deck to the Moon and beyond. Each destination is farther than the last.

Chat Campfire: every message is a log on the fire. A chat keeping its usual pace holds a steady campfire, excitement builds a bonfire, and silence lets it die down to embers.

Options: &sensitivity=low veya high, &scale=1.5, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick, &hud=0 to hide the text, &shake=0 for no shaking, &color=ff8800, ve &align=left, center veya right to move the volcano, rocket or campfire. The seismograph also takes &window=120 for the seconds shown and &bg=0 to drop its panel.

Built for the stream, not the page

Portrait games, team towers and show-time scenes

Seven games drawn to be watched at stream size. They show no titles and no instructions: only the game, the numbers that matter, and the one thing to type.

For portrait streams (9:16)

Peg Drop: yazın: !drop to drop your ball from the moving chute. The pegs decide where it lands, and the gold slot pays triple. Chat Blocks: a falling-block puzzle the whole chat steers. Type a column number, 1 şu değere: 8, to vote where the block lands, or spin to turn it. Sky Drop: yazın: !jump to parachute out of the airship, then left ve right to steer against the wind onto the target. In a 16:9 scene these three keep their portrait shape; add &align=left veya right to move them.

Two teams, clear middle

Tower Duel: every chatter is put on one of two teams automatically. Each message drops a crate from that team's crane, and the first tower to reach the flag wins. In a 16:9 scene the towers use only the sides, so a 9:16 video fits between them untouched.

Before, during a break, and after the show

Front Row: a starting-soon countdown. The first message from each chatter drops them into a seat, and the crowd celebrates at zero. Keep It Up: for breaks. Every message bumps the balloon; chat tries to beat its longest rally. Encore: for the end of the show. Every chatter launches a firework, and a busy chat sets off a finale.

Options: &scale=0.8, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. Peg Drop &round=180 (seconds, 0 for endless). Chat Blocks &turn=8 (seconds to vote). Sky Drop &round=75. Tower Duel &goal=12 (crates to win), &gap=0.3 (share of the width left clear), &panel=0 (no backing), &command=!build (only that command drops). Front Row &minutes=5 veya &until=19:30. Keep It Up &minutes=5 to show a break timer. Encore &crowd=8 (chatters needed for the finale).

Faces first

Races, a boss, a quiz, and a crowd of your chatters

Eleven more pages built around your chatters' own pictures and names. As before: no titles, no instructions, only the game and the one thing to type.

Oynat

Marble Race: yazın: !play and your marble, wearing your picture, races everyone else down an obstacle course that is different every time. Built for portrait. Cliff Climb: every message is one hop up the cliff, and the first to the flag wins. It uses only the two sides of a 16:9 scene, so a 9:16 video fits in the middle. Stream Boss: every message chips at the boss. Tips, subs and raids hit hard, members hit half again as hard, and whoever lands the final blow becomes the next boss, with their own picture in the frame. The boss heals a little whenever they chat. Trivia: a quiz show. Type a, b, c veya d; faster correct answers score more. Ten question packs are built in, or load your own. Stream Bingo: one bingo card for the whole stream, with a picture in every square. When that thing happens, chat types the square's number; enough callers stamp it, and a full line is a bingo.

Always on

Chat Crowd: everyone who chats becomes a little character with their own picture, strolling along the bottom of the stream. They hop when their owner talks, show the first emoji from the message, and obey !jump, !wave, !dance, !hug ve !duel (add a name to pick who).

Before, during a break, and after the show

Roll Call: a starting-soon countdown where everyone who says hello lands on a growing wall of faces. Chat Aquarium: a calm break screen where every chatter is a fish, and a tip drops treasure for the whole tank. Group Photo: an end-of-show class photo. Everyone who says goodbye walks into frame, the camera flashes, and the picture drops out as a print. Winners' Podium: the stream's top players across every game. The games on this page award stars (5, 3 and 2 for the top three of a round, 1 for taking part); the podium adds them up. Chatters type !stars to see their own total. Stars are kept in the browser source's own storage, per session, and start again after twelve quiet hours.

One source, many games

Game Rotation: one browser source that plays a list of games in turn and cross-fades between them. Add &games=marblerace,trivia,pegdrop to choose the list, &every=5 for the minutes each one stays, and &vote to let chat pick the next game by typing 1, 2 veya 3. Every other option on the address is passed on to each game.

Options for every game in this section and the one above: &membersonly (only members and subscribers, and anyone sending a paid message, take part), &scale=0.8, &align=left, &calm (no screen shake or flashes), &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. They slow themselves down on a busy computer rather than slow the stream: first a lower drawing resolution, then fewer frames. &fps=30 veya &quality=0.75 holds a setting, and &fps=full bunu kapatır.

Marble Race &lobby=25 (seconds to join), &max=60 (marbles), &command=play. Cliff Climb &goal=20 (hops to the top), &gap=0.3, &command=climb. Stream Boss &hp=1000 (leave it out and the boss grows with the crowd), &align=bottom left. Trivia &pack=general,science (general, science, geography, history, movies, music, gaming, animals, food, sports), &time=20, &rounds=10, &questions=https://... (your own JSON list of [question, right answer, wrong, wrong, wrong], on a host that allows cross-site requests). Stream Bingo &votes=3 (callers needed; a moderator's call counts at once), &lines=1, &squares=Laughs|Pet appears|.... Chat Crowd &max=30, &idle=10 (minutes before a silent character leaves). Roll Call &minutes=5 veya &until=19:30. Chat Aquarium &minutes=10 (break timer), &idle=10, &water=0 (fish only, over your own break screen). Group Photo &every=45 (seconds between photos), &crowd=12. Winners' Podium &top=8, &season=12 (hours; 0 keeps no scores).

The hype games (Chat Quake, Quake City, Hype Flight, Chat Volcano, Hype Rocket, Chat Campfire) take &skin=winter, spooky, autumn, spring, love veya party for a seasonal look. &membersonly also works on the hype games, the bottom-strip games, Quick Call and the card games further down this page. Tug of War, Rock Paper Showdown, Minority Club, Meteor Shield, Beacon Relay, Crew Kitchen, Sum Squad, Memory Parade and Odd One Out are now drawn as scenes with your chatters' pictures in them; their commands and rules are unchanged.

Halat çekme

Nasıl oynanır?

Yazın: !join ile daha küçük takıma atanırsınız (eşitlikte rastgele). Her iki tarafta da bir oyuncu olduğunda maç başlar. Takımlar maç boyunca sabit kalır; sonradan katılabilirsiniz.

Her 12 saniyede bir kelime veya basit aritmetik sorusu görünür. Kelimeyi veya yanıtı doğrudan sohbete yazın ya da şunu kullanın: !answer ardından yanıtınızı yazın. Her oyuncu soru başına bir çekiş kazanabilir; tekrarlanan yanıtlar ek çekiş sağlamaz.

20 çekiş farkla öne geçen kazanır. Aksi hâlde üç dakika sonunda önde olan taraf kazanır; ip eşitse berabere biter. Sonuç ekranından sonra izleyiciler sonraki maç için yeni takımlara katılır. Sadakat puanı harcanmaz.

Yayına ekleyin

  1. SSN'de şunu açın: Etkileşimli Oyunlar → Oyun Seç.
  2. Yukarıdaki oyunlardan birini seçin ve oturum bağlantısını OBS Tarayıcı Kaynağı olarak ekleyin. 1280 × 900 ile başlayın.
  3. SSN'yi ve sohbet kaynaklarınızı çalışır durumda tutun. Ekrandaki komutu gösterin ve sohbetin turu başlatmasına izin verin.

Rounds advance automatically. To restart a game, refresh its browser source. Add &transparent saydam sayfa arka planı için veya &chroma yeşil için. Şunu ekleyin: &demo only for an isolated preview.

These games use no loyalty points, purchases, external media, or viewer login page. They only read the chosen SSN chat session; they do not send commands back to chat. The same person on two platforms counts as two players. Up to 1,000 distinct players per round.

Şunun için: Ritim Nabzı, set Volume in SSN's Game Options before copying the link. You can also add &volume=0 şu yolla: 100; 0 mutes audio and the default is 50.

Host a phrase game

SSN'de şunu açın: Interactive Games → Phrase Guess host tool. Enter your phrases and select Start Game. Keep this page private; use Dock to show prompts and results to viewers. Try the host preview.

Kelime zinciri daha belirgin skorlar ve okunaklı bir kelime iziyle yenilenmiş bir düzene sahiptir. Bildik kuralları aynıdır: önceki kelimenin son harfiyle başlayarak en az üç harfli alfabetik kelimeleri zincirleyin. Sözlük yerine harf örüntülerini kontrol eder.

Sohbetin ötesinde

Kararı izleyicilere bırakın, siz de uygulayın: sonraki çizim renginizi, kısa bir listeden sonraki şarkıyı veya bir esneme molasını seçsinler. SSN'nin mevcut anketleri, kuyrukları ve yayıncı onaylı çıkartma ödülleri başka katılım yolları sunar. Oturum ve kontrol bağlantılarınızı gizli tutun; izleyicilere yalnızca sohbet yönergeleri gerekir.
