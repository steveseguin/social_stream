How to use them

As a background

  1. Choose one above and adjust it: time of day, weather, colours, how busy it is.
  2. Seçin: Bağlantıyı kopyala and add it to OBS as a Browser Source at your canvas size.
  3. Put it at the bottom of the scene, behind your camera and everything else.

With a title on it

  1. Açın: Starting soon / BRB / ending in Overlay Studio.
  2. Pick any of these under Background, or start from a ready-made style.
  3. Add your title, a countdown and your socials.

Keeping your stream smooth

  1. They draw at 30 frames a second and lower their own resolution if your computer is busy.
  2. Each has a Picture sharpness setting if you want to make it lighter still.
  3. OBS görüntü oluşturma gecikmesi bildirirse animasyonu saniyede 20 kareyle sınırlamak için tarayıcı kaynağının URL adresine &fps=20 ekleyin.
