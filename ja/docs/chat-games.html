配信に重ねて遊ぶ

Firefly Catch and Comet Rally leave the surrounding page transparent; Maze Raid fills its source. Start with a 1280 × 800 browser source. Preview pages show a checkerboard to make the transparent areas visible.

3つの新しい遊び方

Firefly Catch：入力するコマンド： !catch で開始し、ホタルが輪に入ったら捕まえます。各視聴者は飛行1回につき1回挑戦できます。中央で捕まえると3点、輪の中のそれ以外なら1点です。6回の飛行後に勝者が決まります。

Comet Rally：入力するコマンド： !boost で自動割り当てのチームに参加します。各視聴者は3秒に1回ブーストできます。先に20ブーストに達するか、60秒後にリードしているチームが勝ちます。

Maze Raid：自動で探索するキャラクターが、レンガや金属の質感とSSNの看板のある通路を素早く進みます。通常のチャットで、発言者ごとにアバターの敵が1体、1ラウンド最大40体出現します。敵は通路の3～8マス先に現れて追跡・攻撃し、最後の一撃を与えた人が勝ちます。アバター画像がない場合や読み込めない場合はイニシャルが表示されます。迷路はOBSブラウザソース全体に表示されるため、シーンに合わせてソースのサイズを変更してください。

All three restart after an 8-second result screen. No points are spent and no bot messages are sent.

No commands needed

Games that run on hype

Six transparent overlays that react to how excited chat is, not to what it types. Each one measures chat against what is normal for your channel, so a five-person chat and a five-thousand-person chat both get a fair game. Donations, subs, follows and raids count as bigger jolts.

Six ways to spend the excitement

Chat Quake: a live trace along the bottom of the stream. A sudden rush of chat is flagged as a quake, with a magnitude and the word chat was shouting, and the biggest one is kept as the record. Add &style=paper for a paper chart in the corner, or add &theme=night to darken the chart. Inspired by Matthew Busel's ChatQuake.

Quake City: a small skyline along the bottom. Excited chat cracks and topples the buildings one at a time. Flatten it and it is rebuilt a level taller and tougher, so the next knockdown takes more chat.

Hype Flight: chat keeps a little bird in the air through wide, easy gaps. More chat flies higher, the bird ducks under clouds by itself, and only a lull makes it miss.

Chat Volcano: chat builds pressure until it erupts, and the eruption lasts as long as the hype does. The mountain grows after each one.

Hype Rocket: chat fuels a rocket up the side of the screen, from the Cloud Deck to the Moon and beyond. Each destination is farther than the last.

Chat Campfire: every message is a log on the fire. A chat keeping its usual pace holds a steady campfire, excitement builds a bonfire, and silence lets it die down to embers.

Options: &sensitivity=low または high, &scale=1.5, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick, &hud=0 to hide the text, &shake=0 for no shaking, &color=ff8800、および &align=left, center または right to move the volcano, rocket or campfire. The seismograph also takes &window=120 for the seconds shown and &bg=0 to drop its panel.

Built for the stream, not the page

Portrait games, team towers and show-time scenes

Seven games drawn to be watched at stream size. They show no titles and no instructions: only the game, the numbers that matter, and the one thing to type.

For portrait streams (9:16)

Peg Drop：入力するコマンド： !drop to drop your ball from the moving chute. The pegs decide where it lands, and the gold slot pays triple. Chat Blocks: a falling-block puzzle the whole chat steers. Type a column number, 1 → 8, to vote where the block lands, or spin to turn it. Sky Drop：入力するコマンド： !jump to parachute out of the airship, then left および right to steer against the wind onto the target. In a 16:9 scene these three keep their portrait shape; add &align=left または right to move them.

Two teams, clear middle

Tower Duel: every chatter is put on one of two teams automatically. Each message drops a crate from that team's crane, and the first tower to reach the flag wins. In a 16:9 scene the towers use only the sides, so a 9:16 video fits between them untouched.

Before, during a break, and after the show

Front Row: a starting-soon countdown. The first message from each chatter drops them into a seat, and the crowd celebrates at zero. Keep It Up: for breaks. Every message bumps the balloon; chat tries to beat its longest rally. Encore: for the end of the show. Every chatter launches a firework, and a busy chat sets off a finale.

Options: &scale=0.8, &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. Peg Drop &round=180 (seconds, 0 for endless). Chat Blocks &turn=8 (seconds to vote). Sky Drop &round=75. Tower Duel &goal=12 (crates to win), &gap=0.3 (share of the width left clear), &panel=0 (no backing), &command=!build (only that command drops). Front Row &minutes=5 または &until=19:30. Keep It Up &minutes=5 to show a break timer. Encore &crowd=8 (chatters needed for the finale).

Faces first

Races, a boss, a quiz, and a crowd of your chatters

Eleven more pages built around your chatters' own pictures and names. As before: no titles, no instructions, only the game and the one thing to type.

再生

Marble Race：入力するコマンド： !play and your marble, wearing your picture, races everyone else down an obstacle course that is different every time. Built for portrait. Cliff Climb: every message is one hop up the cliff, and the first to the flag wins. It uses only the two sides of a 16:9 scene, so a 9:16 video fits in the middle. Stream Boss: every message chips at the boss. Tips, subs and raids hit hard, members hit half again as hard, and whoever lands the final blow becomes the next boss, with their own picture in the frame. The boss heals a little whenever they chat. Trivia: a quiz show. Type a, b, c または d; faster correct answers score more. Ten question packs are built in, or load your own. Stream Bingo: one bingo card for the whole stream, with a picture in every square. When that thing happens, chat types the square's number; enough callers stamp it, and a full line is a bingo.

Always on

Chat Crowd: everyone who chats becomes a little character with their own picture, strolling along the bottom of the stream. They hop when their owner talks, show the first emoji from the message, and obey !jump, !wave, !dance, !hug および !duel (add a name to pick who).

Before, during a break, and after the show

Roll Call: a starting-soon countdown where everyone who says hello lands on a growing wall of faces. Chat Aquarium: a calm break screen where every chatter is a fish, and a tip drops treasure for the whole tank. Group Photo: an end-of-show class photo. Everyone who says goodbye walks into frame, the camera flashes, and the picture drops out as a print. Winners' Podium: the stream's top players across every game. The games on this page award stars (5, 3 and 2 for the top three of a round, 1 for taking part); the podium adds them up. Chatters type !stars to see their own total. Stars are kept in the browser source's own storage, per session, and start again after twelve quiet hours.

One source, many games

Game Rotation: one browser source that plays a list of games in turn and cross-fades between them. Add &games=marblerace,trivia,pegdrop to choose the list, &every=5 for the minutes each one stays, and &vote to let chat pick the next game by typing 1, 2 または 3. Every other option on the address is passed on to each game.

Options for every game in this section and the one above: &membersonly (only members and subscribers, and anyone sending a paid message, take part), &scale=0.8, &align=left, &calm (no screen shake or flashes), &onlytype=twitch,youtube, &hidetype=kick. They slow themselves down on a busy computer rather than slow the stream: first a lower drawing resolution, then fewer frames. &fps=30 または &quality=0.75 holds a setting, and &fps=full で無効になります。

Marble Race &lobby=25 (seconds to join), &max=60 (marbles), &command=play. Cliff Climb &goal=20 (hops to the top), &gap=0.3, &command=climb. Stream Boss &hp=1000 (leave it out and the boss grows with the crowd), &align=bottom left. Trivia &pack=general,science (general, science, geography, history, movies, music, gaming, animals, food, sports), &time=20, &rounds=10, &questions=https://... (your own JSON list of [question, right answer, wrong, wrong, wrong], on a host that allows cross-site requests). Stream Bingo &votes=3 (callers needed; a moderator's call counts at once), &lines=1, &squares=Laughs|Pet appears|.... Chat Crowd &max=30, &idle=10 (minutes before a silent character leaves). Roll Call &minutes=5 または &until=19:30. Chat Aquarium &minutes=10 (break timer), &idle=10, &water=0 (fish only, over your own break screen). Group Photo &every=45 (seconds between photos), &crowd=12. Winners' Podium &top=8, &season=12 (hours; 0 keeps no scores).

The hype games (Chat Quake, Quake City, Hype Flight, Chat Volcano, Hype Rocket, Chat Campfire) take &skin=winter, spooky, autumn, spring, love または party for a seasonal look. &membersonly also works on the hype games, the bottom-strip games, Quick Call and the card games further down this page. Tug of War, Rock Paper Showdown, Minority Club, Meteor Shield, Beacon Relay, Crew Kitchen, Sum Squad, Memory Parade and Odd One Out are now drawn as scenes with your chatters' pictures in them; their commands and rules are unchanged.

Signal Lock

1?4?

4桁の暗号を90秒で解きます。推測のたびに全員へヒントが出ます。パズル好き、勉強の休憩、ゲームの待機室、小規模コミュニティにぴったりです。

遊び方

入力するコマンド： !code 1234。数字は1から6までで、重複する場合もあります。「Exact」は数字と位置の両方が正しいことを示します。もう一方の数は、数字は正しいものの位置が違う個数です。

最初の有効な推測でタイマーが動き始めます。各視聴者の推測間隔は5秒で、同じ推測は無視されます。

2チーム / 1本の綱

Tug of War

2体の親しみやすいモンスターが、Team CoralとTeam Mintのために綱を引きます。味方が正解すると、綱が自分たちの側へ動きます。

プレビューを試す →

遊び方

入力するコマンド： !join で人数の少ないチームに入ります。同数の場合はランダムです。両チームに1人以上そろうと試合が始まります。試合中のチームは固定ですが、途中参加もできます。

12秒ごとに単語か簡単な計算問題が表示されます。単語や答えをチャットに直接入力するか、次を使います： !answer に続けて答えを入力します。1問につき各プレイヤーが引けるのは1回分だけで、回答を繰り返しても増えません。

20回分の差をつけると勝利です。それ以外は3分後にリードしている側が勝ち、中央なら引き分けです。結果画面の後、次の試合では新しいチームに参加します。ロイヤルティポイントは消費しません。

配信に表示する

  1. SSNで次を開きます： 参加型ゲーム → ゲーム選択（Interactive Games → Select Game）.
  2. 上のゲームを1つ選び、そのセッションリンクをOBSのブラウザソースとして追加します。まずは1280 × 900で試してください。
  3. SSNとチャットソースを起動したままにします。画面上のコマンドを見せて、チャットからラウンドを始めてもらいましょう。

Rounds advance automatically. To restart a game, refresh its browser source. Add &transparent でページの背景を透明にできます。または &chroma で緑色にします。追加するパラメーター： &demo only for an isolated preview.

These games use no loyalty points, purchases, external media, or viewer login page. They only read the chosen SSN chat session; they do not send commands back to chat. The same person on two platforms counts as two players. Up to 1,000 distinct players per round.

対象： リズムパルス, set Volume in SSN's Game Options before copying the link. You can also add &volume=0 経由： 100; 0 mutes audio and the default is 50.

視聴者に選んでもらい、配信者が実行しましょう。次に絵で使う色、候補の中から次に流す曲、ストレッチ休憩などを決められます。SSNの既存の投票、待ち行列、配信者が承認するステッカー報酬でも参加できます。セッションや操作用のリンクは非公開にしてください。視聴者に必要なのはチャットの操作説明だけです。
